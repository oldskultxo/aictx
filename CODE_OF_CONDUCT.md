# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the AICTX community welcoming,
safe, and respectful for everyone.

We expect all participants to act in good faith, treat others with dignity,
and help maintain a collaborative environment regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience,
education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity
or orientation, socioeconomic status, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted attention;
- insulting, derogatory, discriminatory, or demeaning comments;
- sexualized language or imagery where it is not appropriate;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project spaces;
- any other conduct that maintainers reasonably determine to be inappropriate
  in a professional open source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository,
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, documentation, releases, and any
other official communication channels. It also applies when someone is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
standards of behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align
with this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the impact and severity of the behavior, maintainers may take
actions including a private warning, temporary limits on participation,
temporary suspension, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

To report a Code of Conduct concern, use the project issue tracker:

<https://github.com/oldskultxo/aictx/issues>

If the report is sensitive, avoid posting private personal details publicly.
Open a minimal issue requesting maintainer contact, or use any private contact
channel made available by the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers
will respect confidentiality and the safety of all involved parties as much as
practical while resolving the issue.
